## Phys pane and schematic selections cross only within one hierarchy

Selections between the `/phy` pane and the `/sch` schematic are paths rooted at the physical model's top. A `/sch` showing a testbench around the DUT, or another design, selects nothing, with no message. Open the schematic on the synthesis `top:`. See [Physical Metrics](https://rtl-buddy.github.io/rtl_buddy/v6/concepts/phys/#browse-the-model-in-the-hub).
