## Collect coverage

RTL Buddy coverage is currently Verilator-only and follows the platform-selected builder. Use `--builder verilator`, or make Verilator the platform default, instead of relying only on a suite- or test-level override. See [Coverage uses the platform builder](https://rtl-buddy.github.io/rtl_buddy/dev/known-issues/#coverage-uses-the-platform-builder).
